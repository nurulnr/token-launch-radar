    HOW THE VALUE IS WORKED OUT

    A protocol with no token yet is worth what its locked value would be worth if it had one. The implied value is the protocol's TVL times three small multipliers — nothing else, no hand-picked names, no hidden weights.

    Only launch candidates are ranked: a protocol is set aside when it is an exchange, a bridge, a liquid-staking or restaked product, a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades, or a version fork — the same rule the snapshot's own watchlist publishes, applied here without its $50M floor. Set-aside protocols are listed below the board with their reason.

    implied = TVL × growth × age × chains
    • growth — 1 + the 7-day TVL change, capped at −40% to +150%. A protocol growing fast is worth more than its TVL alone.
    • age — 1 + 0.12 × years since listing, capped at 4 years. Older means it survived more than one cycle.
    • chains — 1 + 0.04 × the number of chains, capped at 6. More chains means more reach.

    The same formula runs in app.js as impliedValue(p) — read it there, next to the table code.
